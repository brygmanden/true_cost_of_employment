In Australia, compulsory superannuation currently adds 12% to eligible ordinary wages.

If you’re a medium+ coffee chain, you’ll also be paying payroll tax once you pass the relevant threshold – around A$1 million a year in total payroll.

In the UK, employers generally pay 15% National Insurance on wages above the employer threshold.

Eligible employees may also require a workplace pension contribution on top.

In the US, employers should allow at least another 7.65% on top of wages for Social Security and Medicare, plus unemployment taxes and workers’ compensation insurance.

Health insurance, retirement contributions and other benefits can increase the true labour cost considerably further.
